# Business Case: Duplicate Transaction Investigation

## Background

A bank has received customer complaints about duplicate charges appearing on their accounts.

Customers reported that the same payment was processed more than once for the same merchant and amount.

The QA team was asked to investigate the issue and identify potentially duplicated transactions.

## QA Objective

The goal of this investigation is to:

- identify duplicate transactions,
- validate transaction data consistency,
- analyze relationships between customers, accounts and payments,
- provide findings and recommendations.

## Expected Outcome

A data quality report containing:

- identified issues,
- SQL queries used for investigation,
- root cause analysis,
- recommendations for prevention.
